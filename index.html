About Triumphant Grace

When you look at everything happening around the world today, it can feel tempting to conclude that life has no meaning. Violence, injustice, and every vice imaginable seem to be on the rise, and it can leave the heart heavy with doubt. Yet the Word of God tells us plainly that these very things would mark the season just before the second coming of the Lord Jesus Christ. What looks like chaos to us is, in fact, unfolding within His plan. And even in the midst of these upheavals, the Word of God speaks of hope, an unshakable hope that does not depend on what we see around us. This is the hope I bring to you today, and my prayer is that as you open your heart to Christ through His Word, you will have a life-transforming encounter with Him.
